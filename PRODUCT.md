# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Akram and Aina, a Malay couple planning their engagement (tunang), nikah and wedding. A private two-person tool, used mostly on phones, checked in short sessions to see progress and add tasks.

## Product Purpose
A shared wedding-journey dashboard: countdowns, checklist / wedding / savings progress, upcoming tasks, photobooth memories, and modules for gold savings, tunang, borang checklist, nikah and wedding event planning. Success is the couple knowing at a glance where they stand and what is next.

## Operating Context
Vue 3 + TypeScript + Vite, Firebase backend. Malay wedding terminology (hantaran, nikah, tunang, borang) is factual and must be preserved. Gold savings are tracked in grams.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing routes: /savings, /tunang, /checklist, /nikah, /wedding, /settings. Couple settings (names, dates, love note) come from Firestore.

## Brand Commitments
Warm, personal, romantic voice in copy is kept (e.g. "still counting"). Emoji in headings are to be replaced by SF-style icons.

## Product Principles
- Answer "where do we stand?" in one glance.
- Warm voice, calm interface.
- Phone first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Light with automatic dark mode (follows system).
